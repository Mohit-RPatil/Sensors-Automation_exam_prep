# Important Questions for Sensors & Automation

Here is a list of important questions based on the analysis of previous year papers, syllabus, and assignments. Studying these questions should help you score well in your exam.

## Unit 1: Fundamental of Sensing

*   **Q1:** What is a sensor? Explain its classification and characteristics in detail.
*   **Q2:** What is a transducer? Explain its classification with the help of a block diagram.
*   **Q3:** Differentiate between a sensor and a transducer.
*   **Q4:** Explain the static and dynamic characteristics of a transducer.
*   **Q5:** What are the measurement issues and selection criteria for sensors?

## Unit 2: Motion, Proximity and Ranging Sensor

*   **Q1:** Explain the working principle of LVDT (Linear Variable Differential Transformer) with its three cases and a neat diagram.
*   **Q2:** Explain the working principle of RVDT (Rotary Variable Differential Transformer) in detail.
*   **Q3:** What is a proximity sensor? Explain its types in detail (Inductive and Capacitive).
*   **Q4:** Write short notes on:
    *   GPS (Global Positioning System)
    *   LIDAR (Light Detection and Ranging)
    *   Ultrasonic Sensor

## Unit 3: Force, Magnetic and Heading Sensors

*   **Q1:** What is a strain gauge? Derive the expression for the gauge factor.
*   **Q2:** Explain the working of a capacitive type humidity sensor with a neat diagram.
*   **Q3:** Explain different types of manometer pressure sensors in detail.
*   **Q4:** What is a load cell? Explain the hydraulic and pneumatic load cells with neat diagrams.
*   **Q5:** Write short notes on:
    *   Flow and Level Sensors
    *   Shock and Vibration Sensors
    *   Temperature Sensors

## Unit 4: Advanced Sensor Technologies

*   **Q1:** What is a LASER? Explain its characteristics, types, and working of He:Ne and Nd:YAG LASER.
*   **Q2:** Explain the working principle of a Bar Code Scanner. Discuss the LASER and CCD methods of Bar Code Scanning and its benefits.
*   **Q3:** Explain RFID (Radio Frequency Identification) systems in detail.
*   **Q4:** Write short notes on:
    *   Biosensors
    *   Chemical Sensors

## Unit 5: Industrial Automation

*   **Q1:** What is automation? Explain its needs, advantages, and disadvantages. Also, discuss the different types of automation (Fixed, Programmable, and Flexible).
*   **Q2:** What is the role of a PLC (Programmable Logic Controller) in automation? Explain the block diagram of a PLC.
*   **Q3:** Explain the various elements of automation.
*   **Q4:** Explain Input & Output Modules (AI, DI, AO, DO) in detail.
*   **Q5:** What is ladder logic programming? Explain its components.
*   **Q6:** Write a short note on Distributed Control Systems (DCS) and the role of IoT in industrial automation.
